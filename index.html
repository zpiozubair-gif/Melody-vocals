Questions

Before you press play.

Is Melody Vocals for listeners or singers? +

Both. It's a streaming platform for listeners and a cloud vocal studio for artists in one place — listen, like and queue as a fan, or record, use samples and upload as an artist.

How does the free trial work? +

You get 14 days of Premium free when you join. If you join via someone's invite link, you get 8 extra days — 22 days total. The invite must be used at sign-up.

What is Begin AI? +

Begin is Melody Vocals' always-on AI companion. It greets you when you open the app, asks how you are and what you're working on, and then helps — finding music, organising playlists, or guiding your vocal session.

How do Top Artists work? +

Artists whose songs receive real likes, plays and playlist adds are showcased as Top Artists, with their name, big cover art and top tracks shown clearly — so good voices get discovered.

Can I use it on phone and laptop? +

Yes. Melody Vocals is mobile-first and works beautifully on phone, while the studio and playlists feel roomy and smooth on laptop and desktop too.
